Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Leadzio AI

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in to Leadzio AI using Google Sign-In, we collect your basic profile information (name, email address, and profile avatar) strictly to authenticate your account, manage your credit balance, and prevent trial abuse.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide B2B lead discovery, real-time email verification, and cold outreach automation tools.
  • To track credit usage, referral rewards, and payment verification (via UddoktaPay, bKash, and Nagad).
  • To prevent unauthorized access, automated abuse, and duplicate free-trial claims.

3. Public Business Information & Compliance

Leadzio AI only aggregates publicly available B2B business contact details from public web directories, business websites, and public professional listings. We never sell your personal account credentials or private SMTP passwords to third parties.

4. Payments & Security

All online payments are processed securely via authorized payment gateways (UddoktaPay / bKash / Nagad). We never store your mobile banking PINs or credit card numbers on our servers.

5. Contact & Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting our support team on WhatsApp at 01820575753.
